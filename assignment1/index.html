SCULPTING HARMONY

FRANK GEHRY and the
WALT DISNEY CONCERT HALL

Immediately upon opening the website, I am captivated by all the interacting elements of the page as well as the orchestral background music. Spent a decent amount of time playing around with what results from moving around the mouse at different parts of the page.

Some actions I had noticed during my experience were:
- Text generating as the mouse trails on the title page
- Subtle movements with the imagery and typography — floating effect,
melting sliding type of movement
- Cinematic transitions into the video portions of the web page
- Dynamic page layouts
- First person navigation through the illusion of a three dimensional space

I spent a decent amount of time engaging with The video interviews with Gehry speaking about his small scale models of the building, not only because of the length of the segments but I found it was very interesting to hear about what influenced his ideas, different iterations of the models.

In terms of the elements of the page, exploring all the movements when going down the page was a very engaging aspect of the experience. All the moving parts make the page feel alive.

The layout of each segment moves and changes dynamically as you scroll down the page. Each transition followed the same pattern and transitions were utilised in a way that felt like a guide to the next topic, allowing pieces of information to unfold in a way that is easy to take in.

The interactive experience draws in your attention though visual and audio elements. The style choices reflect the personality of the architecture style that was inspired by the purpose of the concert hall, to hold orchestral performances.

The page is a documentation of the design process that went into creating such a captivating building. In sharing the process, people who appreciate or have experienced the building have a place to go and discover further information about how it came to be.

The sound design is quite an important part of this experience, it really elevates the experience the most. As Gehry talks through his designs they show all the small scale models, a few traditional media design sketches and 3D model designs.

The choice of using orchestral background music is fun and exciting, the elements in classical music are already a powerful tool in keeping your attention. In movies sound plays such a big role in story telling so using music on a website has the same effect.

Despite being fun, engaging and interesting, the interactive elements of the page can be a bit overwhelming. Constant movement and sounds with videos playing can be a lot to take in while navigating pictures and when reading areas with text.
